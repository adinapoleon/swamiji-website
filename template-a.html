About the teacher

A life shaped by practice, service, and inquiry.

The journey began with a sincere question: what remains when the roles, fears, and ambitions of life are seen clearly? That question led to monastic discipline, contemplative study, and the steady companionship of Advaita Vedanta.

Over 27 years of practice, the teaching moved from philosophy into lived experience. Silence became less of an escape and more of a doorway into steadiness, compassion, and clear seeing.

Today, this work is offered to seekers who want honest guidance, disciplined reflection, and a path that honors both ancient wisdom and everyday life.
