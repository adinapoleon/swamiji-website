The story

From renunciation to relationship with the world.

The movement toward monastic life began with a longing for truth that would not quiet down. Years of discipline, study, and service slowly revealed that spiritual maturity is less about withdrawing from life and more about seeing life clearly.

Through 27 years of Advaita Vedanta practice, the teaching became increasingly direct: the peace we seek is not an achievement. It is obscured by misunderstanding and revealed through inquiry.

Now the intention is to help others meet their own questions with honesty, steadiness, and reverence for the mystery at the center of ordinary life.

The work is not to improve the self endlessly. The work is to know what the self truly is.

Three forms of guidance, one essential direction.

The work is private when it needs intimacy, structured when it needs momentum, and communal when it needs shared devotion.
