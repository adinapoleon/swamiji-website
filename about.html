The story

From practice to personal guidance.

The story behind the guidance: a life of study, contemplative practice, and service, shared with sincere seekers.

The path inward

A life shaped by inquiry, discipline, and service.

The movement toward monastic life began with a deep longing for truth. That longing became a call toward renunciation, discipline, scripture, meditation, and service.

In the early years, practice brought structure. It asked for humility, patience, and the willingness to see how much of suffering comes from mistaken identity. Over time, silence became a place for reflection and steadiness.

Teaching that became lived clarity.

Through 27 years of Advaita Vedanta practice, study and inquiry became a way to explore the nature of the self and bring understanding into everyday life.

Now the intention is to help sincere seekers meet their own questions with honesty, tenderness, and precision. Spiritual inquiry can become a sincere, conscious way of living.

A path of inquiry into who we are and how we live.

Bio

Compassion, clarity, and precision.

After a decade in hardware design engineering, Swamiji traveled to India to study Advaita Vedanta, the non-dual wisdom tradition, at the Chinmaya Mission Monastery. For more than two years, that study became a disciplined immersion in inquiry, scripture, contemplation, and service.

He has served the platform of Non-Dual Truth for more than 27 years and now offers that lived experience as a Spiritual Life Coach. In July 2022, he completed 104 hours of training through the Co-Active Training Institute's Coach Training Program.

Bringing together 27 years of monastic life and spiritual practice with 10 years of engineering discipline, Swamiji brings together compassion, clarity, and precision. His guidance invites reflection on life's challenges through self-knowledge and lived experience.
