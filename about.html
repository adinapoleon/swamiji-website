The story

From practice to personal guidance.

Not a resume, but the living story behind the guidance: what led to monastic life, what practice revealed, and why this work is now offered to sincere seekers.

The path inward

A life shaped by inquiry, discipline, and service.

The movement toward monastic life began with a longing for truth that would not quiet down. Ordinary answers could not reach the place where the question lived. That longing became a call toward renunciation, discipline, scripture, meditation, and service.

In the early years, practice brought structure. It asked for humility, patience, and the willingness to see how much of suffering comes from mistaken identity. Over time, silence became less of an escape and more of a doorway into steadiness.

Teaching that became lived clarity.

Through 27 years of Advaita Vedanta practice, the teaching became increasingly direct: the peace we seek is not an achievement. It is obscured by misunderstanding and revealed through inquiry.

Now the intention is to help sincere seekers meet their own questions with honesty, tenderness, and precision. Spiritual life does not need to become another performance. It can become a clear way of living.

The work is not to improve the self endlessly. The work is to know what the self truly is.

Bio

Compassion, clarity, and precision.

After a decade in hardware design engineering, Swamiji traveled to India to study Advaita Vedanta, the non-dual wisdom tradition, at the Chinmaya Mission Monastery. For more than two years, that study became a disciplined immersion in inquiry, scripture, contemplation, and service.

He has served the platform of Non-Dual Truth for more than 27 years and now offers that lived experience as a Spiritual Life Coach. In July 2022, he completed 104 hours of training through the Co-Active Training Institute's Coach Training Program.

Bringing together 27 years of monastic life and spiritual practice with 10 years of engineering discipline, Swamiji offers a rare combination of compassion, clarity, and precision. His guidance helps people meet life's challenges with steadiness, self-knowledge, and a deeper understanding of the life process.
